3. 🔗 Integration Points (Điểm Tích hợp)

💰 Currency Management

  • Document currency (transaction)
  • Company currency (base)
  • Price list currency
  • Automatic exchange rate conversion
  • FX gain/loss calculation

📊 Tax System

  • Sales/Purchase Taxes Template
  • Item-wise tax configuration
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)
  • Tax withholding categories
  • Automatic tax GL entries

💵 Advance Payments

  • Payment Entry với advance allocation
  • Sales Order: advance_paid field
  • Invoice: advances table (FIFO)
  • Outstanding amount calculation
  • Advance return handling

🏢 Internal Transfers

  • is_internal_customer/supplier flag
  • inter_company_order_reference
  • SO ↔ PO automatic linking
  • DN ↔ PR automatic creation
  • Intercompany GL entries

📦 Stock Reservation

  • Sales Order: reserve_stock field
  • Stock Reservation Entry creation
  • Bin.reserved_qty tracking
  • Projected qty calculation
  • Release on delivery/cancel

🔄 Return Handling

  • is_return flag on DN/PR/Invoice
  • return_against linking
  • Reverse stock entries
  • Credit/Debit note creation
  • Outstanding amount adjustment

4. 📊 Key Fields Tracking (Theo dõi Trạng thái)

Sales Order Tracking:

per_delivered
% đã giao hàng, tính từ Delivery Note items
per_billed
% đã xuất hóa đơn, tính từ Sales Invoice grand_total
delivery_status
Not Delivered | Partly Delivered | Fully Delivered | Closed
billing_status
Not Billed | Partly Billed | Fully Billed | Closed
reserve_stock
Giữ hàng cho đơn này (tăng Bin.reserved_qty)
advance_payment_status
Not Requested | Requested | Partially Paid | Fully Paid

Purchase Order Tracking:

per_received
% đã nhận hàng, tính từ Purchase Receipt items
per_billed
% đã ghi sổ, tính từ Purchase Invoice grand_total
receipt_status
Not Received | Partly Received | Fully Received | Closed
billing_status
Not Billed | Partly Billed | Fully Billed | Closed

Invoice & Payment Tracking:

grand_total
Tổng tiền hóa đơn (bao gồm tax)
outstanding_amount
Tiền còn phải thanh toán = grand_total - payments
total_advance
Tổng tiền ứng trước đã allocate
update_stock
Invoice tự động cập nhật kho (skip DN/PR)
is_return
Đánh dấu là Credit/Debit Note (return document)
payment_schedule
Kế hoạch thanh toán theo Payment Terms Template

5. ⏱️ Timeline: Typical Business Flow

Day 1: Customer Inquiry

Khách hàng liên hệ yêu cầu báo giá → Tạo Quotation với items, pricing, và payment terms

Day 3: Customer Accepts

Khách đồng ý → Convert Quotation thành Sales Order → Submit và reserve stock

Day 5: Prepare Delivery

Kho chuẩn bị hàng (có thể dùng Pick List) → Tạo Delivery Note từ Sales Order

Day 6: Ship & Invoice

Submit Delivery Note (giảm stock) → Tạo Sales Invoice từ DN hoặc SO

Day 7-30: Customer Payment

Khách thanh toán theo payment terms → Tạo Payment Entry (Receive) → Allocate vào invoice

Day 30: Order Complete

Sales Order status: Completed | Invoice status: Paid | Stock updated | GL entries balanced
