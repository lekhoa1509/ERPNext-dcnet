🔄 Key Business Flows:
1. Sales Flow:
Lead → Opportunity → Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice → Payment Entry → GL Entry
2. Purchase Flow:
Material Request → RFQ → Supplier Quotation → Purchase Order → Purchase Receipt → Purchase Invoice → Payment Entry → GL Entry
3. Stock Flow:
Delivery Note (Stock Out) / Purchase Receipt (Stock In) → Update Bin (Item × Warehouse) → Stock Ledger Entry → GL Entry
4. Service Flow:
Maintenance Service → Maintenance Visit → Warranty Claim → Issue/Support Ticket → Resolution
