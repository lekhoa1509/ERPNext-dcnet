Luồng Nghiệp vụ Hoàn chỉnh (Cập nhật)

1. Sales Cycle (Bán hàng) - Đầy đủ nhất
Lead
→
CRM: Khách hàng tiềm năng từ marketing/campaigns
Opportunity
→
CRM: Sales opportunity với estimated value
Quotation
→
Selling: Báo giá cho khách, có thể từ Opportunity
Sales Order
→
Selling: Đơn hàng confirmed, reserve stock, trigger các quy trình
Pick List
→
Stock: (NEW) Warehouse picking workflow
Delivery Note
→
Stock: Xuất kho, update stock, có thể tạo Delivery Trip
Sales Invoice
→
Accounts: Hóa đơn bán hàng, tạo GL Entry
Payment Entry
→
Accounts: Thu tiền, reconcile invoice, update outstanding
2. Purchase Cycle (Mua hàng)
Material Request
→
Stock: Yêu cầu mua hàng, có thể từ reorder level
Request for Quotation
→
Buying: Gửi RFQ cho nhiều suppliers
Supplier Quotation
→
Buying: Nhận báo giá từ suppliers, so sánh
Purchase Order
→
Buying: Đơn đặt hàng confirmed
Purchase Receipt
→
Stock: Nhập kho, update stock, Quality Inspection
Purchase Invoice
→
Accounts: Hóa đơn mua hàng, tạo GL Entry
Payment Entry
→
Accounts: Trả tiền supplier
3. Manufacturing Cycle (Sản xuất)
BOM
→
Manufacturing: Bill of Materials, công thức sản xuất
Production Plan
→
Manufacturing: Kế hoạch sản xuất từ Sales Orders
Work Order
→
Manufacturing: Lệnh sản xuất, schedule operations
Job Card
→
Manufacturing: (NEW) Assign operations to workstations
Stock Entry
→
Stock: Material consumption & finished goods
4. Subcontracting Cycle (Gia công) - NEW!
Subcontracting Order
→
Subcontracting: Đơn gia công, gửi raw materials
Subcontracting Receipt
→
Subcontracting: Nhận finished goods từ subcontractor
5. Project & Billing Cycle
Sales Order
→
Có thể tạo Project từ Sales Order
Project
→
Projects: Project management
Task
→
Projects: Task assignment, tracking
Timesheet
→
Projects: Time tracking, billable hours
Sales Invoice
→
Accounts: Billing từ Timesheet

Các Tính năng KHÔNG CÓ trong Diagram cũ

🆕 Features mới (không có trong diagram):

  • Pick List: Warehouse picking workflow cho Delivery Notes
  • Delivery Trip: Multi-stop delivery optimization
  • Shipment: Advanced shipping với tracking (FedEx, UPS integration)
  • Serial and Batch Bundle: Modern serial/batch tracking system
  • Stock Reservation Entry: Reserve stock cho Sales Orders
  • Job Card: Operation-level tracking trong manufacturing
  • Routing: Operation sequences trong production
  • Master Production Schedule: Long-term production planning
  • Production Forecast: Demand forecasting
  • Subcontracting toàn bộ: Order, Receipt, Inward Order, BOM
  • Quality Management module: Review, Procedure, Goal, Action, Non-Conformance
  • Asset Capitalization: Capitalize expenses thành assets
  • Asset Value Adjustment: Adjust asset values
  • POS Invoice: Riêng biệt với Sales Invoice
  • Payment Reconciliation: Advanced payment matching
  • Bank Reconciliation Tool: Auto bank statement matching
  • Invoice Discounting: Factor invoices
  • Deferred Revenue/Expense: Accrual accounting
  • Loyalty Program: Customer loyalty points
  • Coupon Code: Promotional coupons
  • Pricing Rule: Advanced pricing engine
  • Promotional Scheme: Buy X Get Y schemes
